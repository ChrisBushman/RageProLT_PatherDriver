Gouraud (smooth-shaded) triangle rendered on a PowerBook G3 Lombard's ATI Rage
LT Pro under Mac OS X 10.3, by the from-scratch Mach64Accelerator kext.

v0 (top)    = RED    0xFFFF0000
v1 (right)  = GREEN  0xFF00FF00
v2 (bottom) = BLUE   0xFF0000FF

The setup engine interpolated the three vertex colors across the triangle; the
vertex stream (3 packets of {S,T,W=1.0,SPEC,Z,ARGB,X_Y} + ONE_OVER_AREA_UC
trigger) was executed by the chip's bus-master DMA engine reading our command
buffer from RAM.  Each glyph = dominant colour channel of that pixel.
